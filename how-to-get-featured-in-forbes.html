Why Getting Featured in Forbes Actually Matters

Before we get into the how, let's address the why — because many entrepreneurs we speak to aren't sure if Forbes is worth pursuing or just a vanity metric.

A Forbes feature does three concrete things for your business:

  1. It builds instant authority. When a prospect Googles your name and sees a Forbes article, the conversation changes. You go from "who's that?" to "I've heard of them." That shift directly affects your close rate.
  2. It generates a high-authority backlink. A link from Forbes.com is among the most powerful backlinks in SEO. It raises your website's domain authority and strengthens your Google rankings for years.
  3. It becomes a compounding asset. One Forbes feature often leads to more — other journalists who research you will see it and feel safe featuring you too. Media begets media.

The 5-Step Strategy to Get Featured in Forbes

01

Build Your Authority Foundation First

Forbes journalists and contributors don't feature people who have nothing to show. Before you pitch, you need proof of expertise — published content, client results, speaking engagements, or a strong LinkedIn presence. You don't need millions of followers. You need clear, specific positioning. "Business coach" is too broad. "I help 6-figure female founders break through revenue plateaus" is a story Forbes can work with.

02

Find the Right Forbes Contributor for Your Niche

Forbes operates on a contributor model — thousands of writers publish under the Forbes banner, each covering different topics. Find contributors who cover your specific niche: entrepreneurship, coaching, leadership, personal branding. Research their recent articles, follow them on LinkedIn, and engage with their content before you pitch. Cold pitches to strangers get ignored. Warm pitches from someone they recognize get read.

03

Craft a Story-Driven Pitch, Not a Bio

The most common mistake entrepreneurs make is sending a pitch that reads like a press release about themselves. Forbes contributors care about whether your story will interest their readers — not your credentials. Lead with a hook, a conflict, or a counterintuitive insight. "I built a 7-figure consulting business by turning down 90% of clients" is a story. "I am an award-winning coach with 15 years of experience" is a bio. Pitch the story.

04

Time Your Pitch to a Current Trend

Forbes publishes timely content. Tie your pitch to something happening right now — a business trend, a shift in your industry, a surprising data point. "How the rise of AI is changing what clients pay for in coaching" connects your expertise to a news cycle. This makes the journalist's job easier and dramatically increases your chance of a yes.

05

Follow Up — Twice

Most pitches go unanswered not because they were bad but because journalists are buried. A polite follow-up 5–7 days after your initial pitch is standard practice. If you get no response after a second follow-up, move to the next contributor on your list. This is a volume game with quality — pitch 5–10 contributors consistently, not waiting on one.

The 3 Mistakes That Get You Ignored

Mistake 01

Pitching Forbes before you have any credibility signals. If a journalist Googles you and finds nothing, the pitch dies there. Build your online presence first — even a strong LinkedIn profile with 20 posts makes a difference.

Mistake 02

Sending a generic pitch to a mass list of contributors. Contributors talk to each other. A spray-and-pray approach will get you blacklisted faster than rejected. Personalize every single pitch.

Mistake 03

Treating a Forbes feature as a one-time goal rather than an ongoing strategy. The entrepreneurs who compound media coverage pitch consistently, leverage each feature for the next, and treat PR as a business system — not a one-off project.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does it take to get featured in Forbes?

For clients with an established personal brand and strong positioning, we've secured Forbes placements within 6 weeks. For clients starting from scratch, the realistic timeline is 3–6 months to build the foundation and earn the placement. Rushing the process usually produces weak results.

Do you need to pay to be featured in Forbes?

Editorial coverage in Forbes cannot be bought — be wary of any service claiming otherwise. Forbes does offer a paid membership program (Forbes Councils) that allows you to publish under your name, but this is clearly labeled as member content and carries different weight than editorial coverage. What our clients receive is genuine editorial media placement.

Can coaches and consultants get featured in Forbes?

Absolutely — and it's one of the highest-impact moves a coaching or consulting business can make. Forbes regularly features coaches, consultants, and personal brand experts around entrepreneurship, leadership, and personal development. The key is positioning your story around what Forbes readers care about, not just what you do.

What's the difference between a Forbes feature and a Forbes press release?

A Forbes editorial feature is written by a Forbes journalist or contributor and carries the full authority of the Forbes brand. A press release distributed to wire services may display Forbes-adjacent logos but is not the same thing — and sophisticated prospects will know the difference.
